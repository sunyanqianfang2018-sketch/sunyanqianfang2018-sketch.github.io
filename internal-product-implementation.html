01 / Context & contribution

The work in context

WalkMe supports digital adoption within the organisation. My product management work focuses on its implementation and continued use across Asia, Oceania and Africa, connecting market needs with builder enablement, content operations and support.

My focus

I manage the regional implementation of WalkMe: supporting market teams, training content builders, coordinating follow-up and maintaining issues, features and user stories. My scope centres on enterprise implementation and product operations.

  • Market stakeholder coordination
  • Builder enablement
  • Issue and requirement tracking
  • Content operations

02 / Approach

How the work was structured

01

Understand market needs

Run regular market check-ins and awareness sessions to understand questions, issues and potential uses. Translate relevant requests and updates into tracked issues, features and user stories.

02

Enable content builders

Facilitate workshops for new builders, provide learning materials and support follow-up. Help builders move from training to their first publication, with the appropriate publishing access.

03

Support product use

Help teams resolve questions about notifications, content creation and audience setup. Support onboarding and practical demonstrations so new users understand how to access and use the product.

04

Maintain the operating rhythm

Keep content status and builder contacts current, record support activity and coordinate ongoing market communication. Support digital adoption campaigns through the platform.

03 / Design reasoning

What the approach makes possible

Successful implementation needs an operating model around the product. Training, publishing support, market feedback and issue tracking keep local teams connected to the wider delivery effort after initial onboarding.

04 / Outputs & next steps

What the work contributed

  • Builder workshops, learning materials and post-training support
  • Market check-ins, awareness sessions and onboarding support
  • Tracked product issues, features and user stories
  • Maintained content status and builder coordination

Delivery & follow-up
