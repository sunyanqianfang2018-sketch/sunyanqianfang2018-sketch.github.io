01 / Context & contribution

The work in context

Internal product implementation brings together stakeholders, learning needs and the experience of using a product in daily work. My scope sits within enterprise implementation and adoption support.

My focus

My work includes internal product implementation. This public overview focuses on the delivery approach; it does not name the internal product or claim ownership of the full product lifecycle.

  • Stakeholder alignment
  • Enablement planning
  • Content validation
  • Feedback and iteration

02 / Approach

How the work was structured

01

Align the implementation

An implementation starts with a clear problem, the people it is intended to support and an agreed understanding of success.

02

Prepare people and content

Training needs, supporting content and audience considerations form part of implementation readiness.

03

Validate before release

The delivery approach includes checking content and gathering feedback before making it available to its intended audience.

04

Review continued use

Implementation continues beyond release through usage review, content maintenance and identification of further needs.

03 / Design reasoning

What the approach makes possible

Implementation quality depends on the experience around a product as well as the product itself. The public account therefore includes readiness and enablement alongside delivery.

What the work contributed

  • Implementation and adoption support
  • A delivery approach that includes enablement and feedback
