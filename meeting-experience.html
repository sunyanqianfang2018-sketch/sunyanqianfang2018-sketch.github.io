01 / Context & contribution

The work in context

A meeting experience starts before people join and continues after they leave. This work considered virtual, hybrid and in-person settings, including preparation, room booking, participation and follow-up.

My focus

My focus was user research and service design, connecting employee experience with booking guidance and collaborative exploration of a future meeting journey.

  • Prior-research synthesis
  • Interviews and journey activities
  • Survey validation
  • Co-creation
  • Backcasting
  • Service blueprinting

02 / Approach

How the work was structured

01

Build an evidence base

Review earlier research, use pre-interview journey activities and conduct interviews. Examine emerging themes through a follow-up survey.

02

Connect physical and digital touchpoints

Map the meeting journey across preparation, participation and follow-up. Consider inclusion in both the room and the digital experience.

03

Explore a focused service touchpoint

Investigate the booking experience through journey analysis and concept evaluation. Translate the work into guidance on understandable information, room discovery and accessibility.

04

Envision future possibilities

Use co-creation and backcasting to connect a future experience with potential actions. Map employee actions alongside service support and dependencies before discussing feasibility.

03 / Design reasoning

What the approach makes possible

The participant’s experience crosses room facilities, software and working practices. Keeping those elements in one journey helps teams discuss how an improvement in one area affects the rest of the service.

04 / Outputs & next steps

What the work contributed

  • Current-state meeting journeys and personas
  • Booking-experience guidance
  • Future-state journey and service considerations
  • Opportunity and recommendation assessment

Delivery & follow-up

This case brings together research, guidance and envisioning work. Future scenarios are design proposals, not a claim that every capability has shipped; internal feature assessments and vendor discussions remain private.

This account describes collaborative work through my research and design focus. Internal names, participant information, findings, business measures and source materials are omitted. Process descriptions are rewritten for public presentation.
