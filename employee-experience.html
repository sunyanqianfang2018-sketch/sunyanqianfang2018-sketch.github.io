01 / Context

The work behind
the experience.

Within Nestlé’s Global IT UX team, I work on the digital employee experience. The focus is how internal digital products and services support employees in their everyday management and work activities.

02 / My contribution

Where I contributed.

I lead research and adoption work for internal digital products, planning qualitative and quantitative research, synthesising findings and translating user needs into recommendations. As a core member of the Service Design Team, I contribute to work on employees’ end-to-end digital experience.
