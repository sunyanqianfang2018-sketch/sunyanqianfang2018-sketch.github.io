01 / Context & contribution

The work in context

Task management combines personal organisation with shared delivery. The study considered both project work and ongoing operational tasks, examining the experience across the tools and interactions that support them.

My focus

My focus was research and service design: understanding employee journeys and supporting the translation of those journeys into prioritised opportunities with stakeholders.

  • Interviews and journey activities
  • Survey validation
  • Personas
  • Relationship mapping
  • Stakeholder workshops
  • Action prioritisation

02 / Approach

How the work was structured

01

Explore everyday work

Use interviews and pre-session mapping to understand how people manage tasks in context. Validate emerging themes through a survey.

02

Represent different experiences

Develop personas and journeys, then examine how people’s responsibilities and interactions connect across the wider service.

03

Work through opportunities together

Bring research into a stakeholder workshop. Use the journeys to frame questions, discuss possible interventions and identify connections with existing work.

04

Prioritise follow-up

Refine ideas, compare effort and impact, and use voting to support prioritisation. Record next steps and ownership for further action.

03 / Design reasoning

What the approach makes possible

Task management decisions affect coordination between people as much as individual tool use. Combining journey and relationship mapping provides a basis for considering working practices alongside interface changes.

04 / Outputs & next steps

What the work contributed

  • Personas and task-management journeys
  • Stakeholder workshop synthesis
  • Prioritised opportunities and assigned next steps
  • An approach to continued experience measurement

Delivery & follow-up

The research and workshop produced prioritised actions. This public account does not treat those actions as completed rollouts or attribute unverified productivity gains to them.

This account describes collaborative work through my research and design focus. Internal names, participant information, findings, business measures and source materials are omitted. Process descriptions are rewritten for public presentation.
