01 / Design Research Assets

Resources that support a research decision

I develop research resources including intake roadmaps, playbooks, methods and report templates. The aim is to make the purpose of a study clear and help teams retain the connection between evidence and recommendations.

01

Research framing

Structure the decision a study will inform, its audience and scope, and the assumptions that need investigation.

02

Planning and delivery

Use proposals and protocols to connect research questions with appropriate methods and practical activities.

03

Reporting and reuse

Make findings, interpretation and recommendations distinguishable. Support follow-up through clear next steps and reusable research knowledge.

Illustrative example: a research framing prompt

Decision: What will the team be able to decide after this study?

Uncertainty: What do we know, what are we assuming, and what evidence is still missing?

Method: Which observation or conversation can help resolve that uncertainty?

This is a simplified example created for this portfolio, not an internal template.

02 / AI Automation for Research

Automation with a review point

I work on AI automation for research workflows and develop guidance for its use. My approach distinguishes tasks that can be assisted from decisions that require researcher judgement.

01

Assist preparation

Explore support for organising intake information, drafting study material and checking question wording. A researcher still sets the scope and approves the protocol.

02

Support synthesis

Use AI to help structure information and suggest candidate themes. Check them against evidence, including exceptions and contradictory accounts.

03

Review communication

Assist report drafting while keeping evidence, assumptions and recommendations separate. Review certainty and nuance before sharing an output.

Illustrative example: reviewing a suggested theme

A draft theme is a starting point. I would check which observations support it, look for cases that do not fit, and decide whether the wording reflects the evidence.

If support is missing, the theme stays an open question. Fluent wording does not make it a finding.

This example describes a general review principle; it contains no participant data or internal agent instructions.

03 / Validation approach

Quality beyond a polished answer

The guidance covers evidence traceability, question quality, human review and protection of participant information. It also describes comparing outputs when an underlying model changes, so an update is assessed rather than assumed to be an improvement.

  • Check whether a claim is supported by its source.
  • Keep assumptions and missing information visible.
  • Review minority perspectives and conflicting evidence.
  • Reassess quality when the workflow or model changes.

Deliverables & scope

The work includes research resources, an AI use-case playbook and workflow guidance. This public account does not publish internal prompts, agent configurations or company materials, or claim an independently measured efficiency improvement.

The examples on this page are rewritten for public presentation. They are not extracts from an internal playbook or evidence from a participant study.
