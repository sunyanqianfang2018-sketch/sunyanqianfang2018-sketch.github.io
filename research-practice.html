01 / Primary focus

AI Automation in Research

I built a set of task-focused agents to support research planning, knowledge reuse, analysis and communication. My contribution combines agent development, a practical AI Use Case Playbook and workflow design, with researcher review at the points that shape a study or its conclusions.

01

AI Use Case Playbook

I developed a playbook covering the research lifecycle, from initiation and preparation to analysis, reporting and follow-up. It explains where AI assistance can be useful, how to review its output and where researcher judgement remains essential.

02

A set of research agents

The agents support four broad areas of the research process. Their role is to prepare structured material for researchers to assess and use.

  • Planning assistantsOrganise research requests and support study preparation and question review.
  • Knowledge assistantsSurface relevant previous research and identify gaps worth investigating.
  • Analysis assistantsHelp organise research material and develop candidate themes for evidence checking.
  • Communication assistantsSupport report drafting and the refinement of recommendations.
03

Workflow simplification

I designed the flow around reusable context: a reviewed research brief informs preparation, evidence informs analysis, and validated interpretation informs reporting. This helps reduce repeated drafting and makes the handoff between agents and researchers explicit.

Workflow example

Carry context forward

This simplified illustration shows the approach without reproducing internal prompts, configurations or templates.

  1. Frame: organise the research request and identify missing context.
  2. Prepare: use reviewed context to support a proposal, protocol and question review.
  3. Analyse: organise evidence and draft candidate themes for researcher assessment.
  4. Communicate: prepare a report and recommendations with links back to the evidence.
  5. Follow up: retain reusable learning and identify questions for the next study.
Example: reusing context during preparation

A researcher confirms the study objective and audience once, then carries that reviewed context into the proposal and discussion guide. An agent can assist with drafting; missing information remains visible for the researcher to resolve.

This is a portfolio illustration, not a live agent or a copy of an internal workflow.

Validation approach

Review evidence, then use the output

I build validation into the workflow: checking source support, missing context, question quality and the distinction between an observation and its interpretation. Changes to a model or agent are assessed by comparing outputs before wider use.

  • Check candidate themes against supporting and conflicting evidence.
  • Keep assumptions and unanswered questions visible.
  • Review wording for leading or ambiguous questions.
  • Keep final research decisions with the researcher.

Deliverables

An AI Use Case Playbook, research agents and workflow guidance. This account describes the work and its intended benefits without claiming an unverified time saving or a fully autonomous research process.

02 / Supporting practice

Research Scale Up

Alongside automation, I work on making research easier for teams to plan, conduct and reuse. This includes shared methods, intake roadmaps, report templates and UX maturity workshops.

My desk research explores how research operations, supported participation by non-researchers and reusable knowledge can help a research practice grow. These inform the approach; they are not a claim that every proposed operating model has been implemented.

  • Shared processes that connect a research request to a decision.
  • Guidance and review for teams contributing to research.
  • Reusable learning that can inform subsequent studies.

Descriptions and examples are rewritten for public presentation. Internal agent instructions, configurations, source documents and participant data are not published.
