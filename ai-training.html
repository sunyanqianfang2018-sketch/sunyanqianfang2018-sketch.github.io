01 / Context & contribution

The work in context

Introducing AI into everyday work requires learning support as well as access. My work spans global and regional adoption initiatives, with a focus on helping people understand and use AI in their work.

My focus

I facilitate workshops and training, develop training content, and monitor usage and adoption as part of the enablement work.

  • Workshop facilitation
  • Training facilitation
  • Learning-content development
  • Usage and adoption monitoring

02 / Approach

How the work was structured

01

Facilitate workshops

Create space for participants to explore AI use in a work context, with guided discussion and opportunities to ask questions.

02

Develop learning content

Prepare material that explains use cases and supports practical learning. Keep content understandable for the audience rather than assuming technical familiarity.

03

Deliver training

Facilitate learning sessions and support participants as they work through the material.

04

Follow adoption

Monitor usage and adoption as part of the wider programme, alongside the ongoing work of training and enablement.

03 / Design reasoning

What the approach makes possible

Research and training serve different purposes. Research helps establish what needs to be understood; training helps people develop their ability to use a tool. I keep those responsibilities distinct while connecting them within adoption work.

What the work contributed

  • Ongoing usage and adoption monitoring
